01 INFRARED SAUNA THERAPY

Not just a hotter room

Radiant heat that meets you where you are.

Traditional saunas heat the air around you. Infrared saunas use panels to emit radiant energy that warms your body more directly, typically at a lower ambient temperature.

That means a calmer, more approachable session for many people, with the same simple invitation: make space to breathe, reset, and let your body warm from within.

Choose your wavelength

Start gently

Your first session, simplified.

Infrared sauna use should feel restorative, never like a test of endurance.

01

Begin with 10–15 minutes at a comfortable temperature.

02

Drink water before and after your session, and listen to your body.

03

Build gradually as your comfort grows. Consistency matters more than intensity.

A considered noteConsult your healthcare professional before using an infrared sauna if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or take medication affected by heat.
