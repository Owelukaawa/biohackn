The science of light

Red light. Better recovery.

Red light therapy is being used by athletes, fitness professionals, and wellness-focused routines to support recovery, promote cellular function, and help the body feel more ready for the next session.

01 / RED LIGHT

Why athletes use it

Support your recovery without adding more stress.

Red light therapy is a non-invasive recovery tool that uses specific wavelengths of light to support cellular function. The goal is not to “force” performance, but to help the body repair, recover, and feel more ready between sessions.

For athletes and active adults, that means better alignment with training demands, reduced soreness in some cases, and a more sustainable recovery routine that fits around everything else.

What it targets

Three ways light supports the body.

Red and near-infrared wavelengths are used because they can reach deeper tissue and support the body’s natural recovery pathways.

01 R Red light

Often used for surface-level recovery, skin support, and encouraging an environment that is more conducive to repair.

Cell support
02 NIR Near-infrared

Penetrates deeper into tissue and is commonly paired with red light to support muscle recovery and cellular energy production.

Deeper recovery
03 ATP Cellular energy

By supporting mitochondrial activity, red light may help cells function more efficiently during repair, recovery, and readiness.

How to use it

Make the habit consistent.

Red light therapy works best when it fits naturally into a routine. Most people use it before or after training, or as part of a wind-down ritual in the evening.

The key is to use it regularly rather than expecting a single session to create dramatic change. Better outcomes usually come from repetition, consistency, and a well-rounded recovery plan.

  • Use it around sessions or recovery windows for best routine fit.
  • Build sessions around your actual schedule so they are easy to repeat.
  • Pair it with sleep, hydration, and stress management for stronger results.

Important note

Use it intelligently.

Red light therapy is generally considered a low-risk recovery tool, but it is still smart to choose a quality device and follow the manufacturer guidance. The right dose and treatment area matter just as much as the wavelength.
