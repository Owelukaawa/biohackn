Products and wellness

What products does Biohackn offer?

We offer premium wellness technologies including infrared saunas, cold plunges, red light therapy panels, hyperbaric systems, PEMF devices, blue-light blocking glasses, hydrogen water products, and other wellness essentials.

Which product is right for my goals?

It depends on your goals, space, routine, and experience. Infrared saunas are often chosen for heat-based relaxation and recovery, cold plunges for cold exposure and post-training recovery, red light therapy for convenient daily sessions, and hyperbaric systems for specialized oxygen-based wellness routines. Our team can help you compare options.

Are Biohackn products medical treatments?

Biohackn products and website content are provided for general wellness and informational purposes. They are not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning a new wellness routine, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication.

Do products include setup or usage guidance?

Product-specific instructions and support vary by item. Before using any equipment, read the supplied instructions and follow the recommended setup and safety guidance. Our support team can help with general product questions and next steps.

Orders and delivery

How do I place an order?

Browse our wellness solutions, open the product you are interested in, and follow the checkout steps. If you would like help selecting a product before ordering, contact our team through WhatsApp.

Where does Biohackn deliver?

Biohackn serves customers across Dubai and the UAE. Delivery availability, timing, and installation requirements can vary by product and location, so we recommend confirming details with our team before ordering larger equipment.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timing depends on the product, stock status, destination, and any required logistics or installation. The team will confirm the expected timeframe for your order. Delays can occasionally occur because of circumstances outside our control.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order details. We will do our best to help, but changes or cancellations may not be possible once an order has been processed, dispatched, or prepared for installation.

Returns and support

What is your return or refund policy?

Returns, exchanges, cancellations, and refunds depend on the product and the applicable store policy. Contact us before sending anything back so we can confirm eligibility, required documentation, and the next steps.

What if my order arrives damaged or incomplete?

Contact us promptly with your order number and clear photos of the packaging and product. Please keep the original packaging until we confirm the resolution. We will review the issue and guide you through the available options.
