01 HYPERBARIC OXYGEN THERAPY

The core idea

Pressure changes how oxygen moves through the body.

In a hyperbaric chamber, air pressure rises above normal atmospheric pressure while the user breathes concentrated oxygen. The result is more dissolved oxygen in plasma, allowing oxygen to reach tissue beyond what red blood cells carry alone.

That mechanism is distinct from simply breathing oxygen at normal pressure. It is a clinical technology first, with wellness applications that should be discussed with a qualified provider rather than treated as a cure-all.

Where it may fit

A system for oxygen delivery, not instant transformation.

Evidence and protocols vary by condition. The strongest decisions start with a clear goal, a qualified assessment, and realistic expectations.

Safety is part of the system

Pressure therapy deserves professional screening.

Hyperbaric oxygen is not suitable for everyone, and the chamber environment adds risks that ordinary wellness equipment does not.

01

Complete a medical screening, especially for ear or sinus problems, lung disease, pregnancy, medications, or untreated pneumothorax risk.

02

Use a trained operator and follow the chamber's oxygen, fire-safety, pressure, and emergency procedures exactly.

03

Stop and report ear pain, breathing difficulty, vision changes, unusual fatigue, or other unexpected symptoms.
